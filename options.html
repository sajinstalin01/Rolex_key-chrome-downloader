Privacy & limitations

  • History, metadata snapshots, and theme settings stay in Chrome local storage.
  • CRX downloads are checked for CRX magic/header bounds and SHA-256 is calculated before download.
  • Manifest analysis runs locally; its static risk flags are not a malware verdict.
  • Selected folders for ZIP export are processed locally.
  • CRX availability and store metadata depend on Google's endpoints and page structure.
